# Differentiated Existence Model
## Working Thesis

**Status:** developing foundational framework and research program. This document is not a claim that DEM is experimentally established, peer reviewed, or a completed Theory of Everything.

## Abstract

The Differentiated Existence Model (DEM) begins with the least committal premise available: **existence**. It then asks what can be constructed from **differentiation** without quietly promoting the products of measurement, notation, coordinate choice, partition, or resolution into primitives of reality.

DEM's central methodological claim is that a boundary appearing in a model may be a boundary introduced by the act of resolving the model. Locations, objects, events, geometric distance, temporal labels, information, causation, force, and energy are therefore treated as candidates for derived descriptions of deeper correlated differentiation rather than assumed primitive substances.

The framework permits, but does not yet establish, several stronger hypotheses: dimensions need not all be geometric or fixed; dimensional participation and metric relationships may vary; time may be an ordered metric serialization of resolved change rather than an ontological dimension of the same kind as geometry; and the invariant speed `c` may characterize causal propagation inside a spacetime-resolved regime without logically constraining every possible relation in existence.

Any physical theory built from DEM must reproduce the successful quantitative predictions of relativity, quantum mechanics, quantum field theory, and statistical mechanics in their tested domains.

---

## 1. Method: build from what must be admitted

DEM deliberately avoids beginning with space, time, particles, information, fields, laws, or observers. Each of those concepts already contains substantial structure.

The working discipline is:

1. Admit **existence**.
2. Admit only as much **differentiation** as is required for non-coincident designation.
3. Introduce further structure only when it is logically required, empirically required, or explicitly marked as a hypothesis.
4. Treat every hard boundary as suspect until shown to be invariant rather than resolution-created.
5. Preserve established physics as an effective description wherever it is experimentally successful.

This is the **anti-smuggling rule**: do not silently turn a coordinate, partition, measurement convention, or stabilized representation into fundamental ontology.

## 3. Core definitions

### Existence

The encompassing category of whatever exists. DEM does not define existence using space, time, matter, information, or objects, because doing so would import later structure into the starting point.

### Differentiation

The availability of non-coincident designation within some mode of variation, without requiring a primitive hard boundary between the designations.

### Dimension

A mode or degree along which differentiation can be designated. A dimension need not be geometric.

### Resolution

An interaction, model, or measurement context that distinguishes and stabilizes some structure while not necessarily exhausting the underlying structure.

A candidate formal expression is:

```text
R_ρ : E → S_ρ
```

where `E` is underlying existence, `ρ` labels a resolution/interaction context, and `S_ρ` is the structure distinguishable under that context.

### Change

Differential expression along dimensionality under differing conditions. This definition does not require primitive time, a traveling object, or an assumed causal arrow.

### Correlation

A relationship among resolved differentiations or changes, potentially within or across dimensions, without presuming causal direction.

### Object

A stabilized resolved bundle of correlated multidimensional differentiation/change. Persistence does not require a fundamental hard-bounded thing.

### Event

A resolution-defined grouping or continuity of correlated multidimensional change, useful for observation and calculation but not currently treated as primitive.

## 4. Dimensional participation and metric structure

DEM distinguishes between:

```text
participates(G3) = false
```

and

```text
location(G3) = 0
```

The first says an expression does not resolve along a dimension; the second already assumes participation and assigns a coordinate.

DEM permits the hypothesis that dimensional participation itself can change. It also permits metric relationships among resolved dimensions to vary.

A minimal toy representation is:

```text
ds_G = a_G(q) dq
ds_D = a_D(q) dq
ρ_GD(q) = a_G(q) / a_D(q)
```

where `q` is an underlying change parameter not assumed to be physical time. A physically meaningful dimensional-stretch hypothesis would require an invariant observable for `ρ_GD`, not merely a coordinate reparameterization.

## 5. Time

DEM's working hypothesis is:

> **Time is the ordered and metric serialization of resolved change or state comparison, rather than a fundamental dimension of the same ontological kind as geometric modes.**

Operational clocks already measure one repeatable physical process against another. DEM asks whether the temporal coordinate used in successful physical theories is an exceptionally powerful resolved bookkeeping structure rather than an ultimate ontological axis.

This hypothesis has a hard requirement: DEM must recover Lorentz transformations, relativity of simultaneity, proper time, time dilation, gravitational redshift, and relativistic field dynamics quantitatively. A naive universal global sequence would not be compatible with observed relativity.

## 6. Speed and the status of c

Speed is downstream of both geometry and clock comparison:

```text
v_R = d_G,R / dτ_R
```

DEM fully accepts the experimentally established invariant role of `c` in relativistic spacetime. Its narrower hypothesis is that `c` may be an invariant of a particular resolved Lorentzian relationship rather than a logically necessary bound on every possible mode of differentiation in existence.

This does **not** amount to a claim that ordinary matter or information has been observed traveling faster than light.

If a new controllable channel ever allowed information selected after spacelike separation to arrive outside the standard light cone, that would operationally count as faster-than-light communication regardless of the deeper ontology used to explain it.

## 7. Information, causation, force, and energy

DEM avoids treating information as a ghostly substance. Operational information is a resolvable distinction under an interaction, decoder, or basis.

Causation is provisionally treated as a directional interpretation of structured correlation/change under a chosen ordering and partition. This does not reject causal modeling; it asks whether the arrow labeled `cause → effect` is fundamental or emergent.

Likewise:

- **Energy** may be a quantitative readout of resolved multidimensional correlated differentiation rather than primitive substance.
- **Force** may be the resolved measure of correlated redistribution/change in dimensional expression rather than an invisible pushing material.
- **Momentum** is an immediate comparison target for any deeper state quantity because generalized momentum already exists for generalized coordinates.

These are hypotheses, not replacements for the established mathematical definitions of energy, momentum, force, or stress-energy.

## 8. Candidate propensity

DEM currently uses **propensity** as a provisional name for a possible less-resolution-dependent quantity encoding the capacity of correlated differentiation to express as change across available dimensions.

A candidate projection picture is:

```text
p_i = projection(P, e_i(R))
```

where `P` is a deeper vector/covector/tensor-like state quantity and `e_i(R)` is a resolution-dependent basis. Then an observed component could change because `P` changes, the basis/metric changes, or both:

```text
dp_i = d[projection(P, e_i)]
```

Before DEM is allowed to treat propensity as new physics, it must be tested against generalized momentum, action, Hamiltonian flow, stress-energy, quantum-state invariants, and related established structures. If it merely renames an existing quantity without adding explanatory or predictive power, the term should be discarded.

## 9. Relation to established physics

DEM is intentionally an **enclosure** proposal rather than a rejection proposal. Successful physical theories must remain successful inside their tested regimes.

A viable formal DEM must at minimum recover:

- Lorentz covariance and relativistic causal structure;
- proper-time and gravitational clock effects;
- general-relativistic curvature in the tested limit;
- Born-rule probabilities, interference, and superposition;
- Bell-inequality violations without falling back to Bell-local hidden variables;
- standard no-signalling marginals unless DEM makes a new explicit, testable prediction;
- quantum-field behavior, particle statistics, scattering, antimatter, gauge structure, and conservation laws;
- thermodynamic/statistical behavior and the observed arrow of records.

Ontology may change. Tested predictions may not simply be discarded.

## 10. Candidate physical realization: coherent-fluid and vortex models

A newly identified research connection is **Superfluid Vortex Theory (SVT)**, an independent speculative program that models the vacuum as a coherent quantum fluid and interprets particles as stable vortex structures, gravity as an emergent acoustic geometry, and larger-scale phenomena using related vortex dynamics.

DEM does **not** adopt the claim that existence *is* a superfluid. Doing so would violate the anti-smuggling rule by promoting one candidate resolved description directly into ontology.

Instead, DEM can ask a narrower and testable question:

> Could a coherent-field / vortex theory provide one dynamical realization of the DEM idea that objects are stabilized correlated structures and spacetime geometry is emergent under a particular resolution?

This possibility is interesting because a stable vortex is exactly the kind of persistent relational structure that could instantiate DEM objecthood without requiring a primitive particle-container.

The correspondence to investigate is:

```text
Differentiated existence
    ↓ resolution/dynamics
Correlated field modes
    ↓ stabilization/topology
Persistent vortex configuration
    ↓ physical resolution
Particle-like object
```

This is a research hypothesis, not evidence for either DEM or SVT.

## 11. Fluid singularities as resolution failure

OpenAI's September 2026 proposed solution of the three-dimensional Navier–Stokes existence-and-smoothness problem provides a mathematically concrete example of a continuum description being driven to a finite-time singularity. The proposed construction uses an inward-spiraling, increasingly elongated vortex whose continuum velocity becomes unbounded while energy remains finite.

DEM suggests a general interpretive possibility:

> A mathematical singularity can sometimes indicate failure of a resolution or effective description rather than an infinity in underlying existence.

For an ordinary molecular fluid, the continuum approximation must eventually give way to microscopic degrees of freedom. For a hypothetical quantum-fluid vacuum, a corresponding question would be whether quantum pressure, finite core size, topology, or other microscopic structure regularizes analogous continuum focusing.

This connection gives a concrete mathematical research target without assuming that the vacuum actually is a superfluid.

## 12. What would count as progress

DEM must eventually do at least one of the following:

1. derive familiar physical laws from fewer independent assumptions;
2. unify structures currently introduced separately;
3. predict a measurable deviation from existing theories.

Purely verbal reinterpretation is not enough.

## 13. Falsification and failure modes

Strong DEM claims would be seriously damaged by, among other things:

- equations that fail precision tests of relativity or quantum mechanics;
- a proposed dimensional-stretch effect removable entirely by coordinate change;
- a propensity variable with no content beyond renaming known quantities;
- an FTL claim reducible to shared randomness, leakage, synchronization error, postselection, or ordinary Bell correlations;
- a universal-present model unable to reproduce relativistic clock effects and simultaneity structure;
- an entanglement model reducible to Bell-local hidden variables;
- a boundary-skeptic principle unable to accommodate real discrete spectra or topological invariants.

## 14. Current thesis

The strongest formulation DEM can responsibly make today is:

> **Start with existence. Admit differentiation. Construct everything else cautiously, and never mistake a stabilized resolution of differentiated existence for existence itself.**

The next stage is mathematical. DEM must identify the least mathematical structure able to represent variable dimensional participation, define a resolution operator precisely, recover an effective Lorentzian regime, produce stable object-like structures, and expose at least one invariant empirical discriminant.
